Travel Resources

How to choose a trustworthy travel company

Check that the company shows a verified registration, a real office address and a clear cancellation policy. Compare itineraries line by line, not just the headline price.

Kedarnath yatra checklist

Carry a rain jacket, warm layers, a valid photo ID, your yatra registration and any regular medicines. Book stays before the peak season from May to June.

How the Trust Score works

Each company is scored out of 100 across 9 parameters, including itinerary quality, safety, transparency and support, after documents, mystery bookings and customer feedback are checked.

Spotting hidden charges

Ask what is not included: entry fees, permits, heater or oxygen charges, and pony or helicopter costs. A trustworthy company lists inclusions and exclusions in writing.

Best time to visit popular destinations

Kedarnath and Spiti are best from May to October, Leh Ladakh from June to September, Kerala from September to March and Bali from April to October.

Travel safety basics

Share your itinerary with family, keep digital copies of documents, buy travel insurance and confirm the operator's emergency contact before you leave.
